# US Health Partners, LLC

> US Health Partners (USHP) is a physician-led national cardiovascular care network headquartered in Rockville, Maryland. USHP partners with independent cardiology practices to advance cardiovascular care delivery, combining clinical innovation and management support while practices remain independent.
