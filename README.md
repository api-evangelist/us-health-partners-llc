# US Health Partners, LLC

US Health Partners (USHP) is a physician-led national cardiovascular care network headquartered in Rockville, Maryland. USHP partners with independent cardiology practices across the country, providing management support, clinical governance, and technology while practices remain independent — reporting roughly 800 team members, 525K patients, and 30 locations.

Backed by: bessemer-venture-partners, summit-partners, silversmith-capital-partners — https://www.ushphealth.com/

USHP publishes no public developer portal, API, or machine-readable discovery surface as of July 2026 (well-known paths and developer subdomains probed; none exist).
